Privacy and beta information

This free public beta provides template-based viva practice and heuristic feedback. AI chat is not activated, and scores do not predict marks or assess factual correctness.

Your data

Your report, current answer draft and practice state are stored unencrypted in this browser’s localStorage. Completed sessions are stored in IndexedDB, up to 50 sessions. History contains questions, answers, title and heuristic scores; questions can quote report excerpts. Nothing synchronizes to another device. Avoid confidential information and shared devices.

Voice

Local recordings stay in tab memory and are discarded when you change questions or close the page. Read-aloud uses an available local English voice. Optional browser dictation requires consent for each question and may send microphone audio to your browser vendor’s speech service under its policies. It may require an internet connection. This site does not upload recordings itself.

Deletion and export

Use Clear local data to remove the current practice report, answers and saved sessions. History also supports individual and bulk deletion. Export JSON copies before clearing browser data; browser storage can be removed or evicted. Exported files remain your responsibility to keep or delete.

Hosting and limitations

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process request metadata under GitHub’s policies. This beta has no accounts, payments or application analytics. Real-device microphone and speech recognition behavior varies. Review all guidance critically and follow your institution’s academic integrity requirements.
